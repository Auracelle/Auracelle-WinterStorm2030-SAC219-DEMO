WinterStorm2030

Stress-testing NATO's governance capacity across the grey zone.

Today NATO faces a growing set of challenges across the Arctic High North. Blue Team analysts are operating in the grey zone — pressure from Russia and China that stays deliberately below the threshold of open conflict — and need to explore scenarios they haven't seen yet, not just refight familiar ones. WinterStorm2030 exists to stress-test both: the known scenarios and the unknowns. Every session tracks a single live number, the NATO Capability Gap (Cap Gap — the deficit between allied governance capacity and what the threat actually requires), as it responds to each move you and the adversary make.

Classical QUBO/Ising Optimizer — Quantum-Enhanced E-IAIG-HT analog
QUBO // Ising Hamiltonian // Simulated Annealing
CLASSICAL SIMULATION — NOT QUANTUM HARDWARE
What this panel does / the math it's based on
Per the Quantum-Enhanced E-IAIG-HT provisional (filed 08/07/2026, related to App #64/108,262), the governance capacity gap is Δ = g-GWC_max − g-GWC_current, where the Stratum III composite is g-GWC = (1/8)·Σj [w_j · BGC_j · (1 + ACC_j·AI_readiness_j)] across the 8 BGCs (STI, SAD, ESI, NDM, SRA, IIC, ASI, CD). Closing Δ is formulated as a Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization (QUBO) over concession-trade decision variables xi,j,k, converted to an Ising Hamiltonian H(s) = Σi hisi + Σi<j Jijsisj and, in the patent's preferred embodiment, submitted to a quantum annealer (e.g. D-Wave Advantage) or QAOA circuit — the full formulation covers the 9-actor / 216-variable Arctic actor set.
SCOPE OF THIS PANEL — Runs a real, in-browser classical simulated-annealing solve of the same QUBO structure — a reduced 15-variable instance (the 5 Blue actors above × 3 concession types), αi,j seeded from the Actor Analysis g-GWC baselines and the live Concession Engine weights (Access 4 / Resource 3 / Narrative-Legal 2), and a mutual-exclusivity penalty encoding "one concession per actor." This is the classical heuristic the patent's own Background section names as the thing quantum annealing is meant to outperform on the full 216-variable problem.

Classical QAE Analog — Stratified-Sampling Governance State Projection
Section VI of the Quantum-Enhanced E-IAIG-HT spec
CLASSICAL SIMULATION — NOT QUANTUM HARDWARE
What this panel does / the math it's based on
Per Section VI: classical Monte Carlo converges as O(1/√N) — 4× the samples to halve the error — while QAE estimates the same outcome probability using Grover's operator and phase estimation in roughly √N queries; the spec's own example is 2,000 classical samples ≈ 45 QAE queries for comparable accuracy. This panel reuses the exact Monte Carlo engine and inputs above and compares three estimators of P(Win-Win) / P(Critical Breach) side by side.
SCOPE OF THIS PANEL — There is no classical substitute for genuine quadratic quantum speedup — Grover-based amplitude estimation needs an actual quantum circuit. What runs here is the closest honest classical analog: stratified sampling on the dominant source of variance (Red's first-move tag), which — like QAE — squeezes more accuracy out of a small sample by removing a known source of noise rather than averaging over it. It demonstrates the "fewer samples, comparable accuracy" shape of the claim; it does not achieve true quadratic scaling.
